# Late-frame investigation

The historical six-process RustDesk soak accounted for 49,832 = 49,827 drawn + 5 late. Its one-second metrics place two losses in specimen 1 near media 197 and 292 s, two in specimen 3 near 28 s, and one in specimen 4 near 28 s. Three losses share one host-second across concurrently launched players. No seek ran; these are not first-frame losses. Packet inspection finds 7 ms intervals in specimens 1/3 and 9 ms in 4. The scheduler retires expired intervals before output submission; submitted equals drawn. These facts do not distinguish scheduler delay, decode availability, and outstanding output-credit delay for each historical frame. No exact inherent five-frame bound is claimed. Per-frame attribution remains deferred; the retained aggregates cannot recover it.

The mixed route had a separate reproducible scheduling limitation: only audio callbacks consumed its video due tick. The worker now waits on that deadline directly, while keeping the audio clock authoritative. Audio-less playback retains its bounded heartbeat; mixed playback gains no idle heartbeat. Tick-to-nanosecond conversion rounds upward with integer arithmetic. The callback and third-party allocation paths are unchanged.

An isolated repeat of the 987-frame Opus remux measured **968 drawn + 19 late before**, **979 drawn + 8 late after**. Both rendered exactly 1,995,408 audio frames, zero clock-advancing underruns, no native failure, and clock rate approximately 1.0000. This is a measured improvement, not an all-drawn or deterministic residual bound. The historical mixed run's 14 late frames are not reclassified as drawn. See the complete before/after metrics and identities in adjacent directories.

The regression test with a real host deadline and no audio callbacks passes; restoring the old mixed-route exclusion makes it fail at the deadline assertion. The source is restored byte-identically; `deadline-revert-proof.json` retains fixed/reverted/restored exit codes 0/1/0. No renderer tolerance, lateness allowance, queue reservation, or clock policy was widened.
